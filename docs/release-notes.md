# Release Notes

## Version 2026.11.16-3 — Production release

### Highlights

- Thanksgiving arcade gameplay: fly a turkey and deliver mashed potatoes to tables.
- Autumn village scenery with Thanksgiving guests.
- Directional gobble blast, crows, a dirty-dishes monster, and guardian-angel shields.
- Original arcade-style instrumental music inspired by “America the Beautiful” and a turkey gobble sound effect.
- Local top-ten high scores and separate music and sound-effect controls.
- Touch controls for flying, firing, and dropping deliveries.
- No account, advertisements, analytics, or online leaderboard.
